About me

I'm Amos. I keep it real, respectful and a little aesthetic.

A passionate gamer and sports kid from Kerala. I love good people, good food, and the open road.

Loves to talk. Loves to listen more. I won't even exist in the room if there's disrespect in the air — culture, people, small details, all of it matters to me.

Most days you'll find me on Minecraft, on a pitch, or driving somewhere with the windows down. The rest of the time, I'm eating ghee rice and pretending I'll stop after one plate.
